# Amazon, Youth, and Climate Resilience — Instituto Gira Studies

Repository of research, data analysis, and territorial studies developed within the scope of **Instituto Gira**, focusing on the impacts of climate change on Amazonian youth, socio-environmental vulnerability, and local adaptation strategies.

## 📌 Context & Relevance (HARMONIZE Alignment)
This portfolio compiles empirical and territorial evidence from the Amazon region, bridging local youth perspectives with climate and environmental data to support policy-making and climate-health resilience.

## 📂 Repository Structure
- **`/reports`**: Policy briefs, diagnostic reports, and publications produced with Amazonian youth.
- **`/data`**: Structured datasets, survey summaries, and metadata dictionaries (anonymized for ethical compliance).
- **`/scripts`**: Data processing, statistical analysis, and spatial mapping scripts.
- **`/visualizations`**: Key charts, territorial maps, and indicators.

## 🔍 Featured Studies
1. **[Policy Brief I: Climate Adaptation, Mitigation, and Amazonian Youths: Postponing the End, Building Today]**: Analyzes 511 climate emergency decrees across the Legal Amazon (2020–2025), highlighting how extreme weather and environmental racism disproportionately impact youths and proposing a participatory adaptation program.
2. **[Policy Brief II: Just Transition in the Amazon: Possible Structures for the Inclusion of Brazilian Youths]**: Evaluates 627 state environmental policies and surveys 667 youths, critiquing reactive governance and fossil fuel expansion while proposing structures to shift Amazonian youth from symbolic inclusion to real decision-making power.

## 🛠️ Methodologies & Tools
- Participatory action research with Amazonian youth networks
- Climate and socio-environmental data analysis (ex: QGIS, Python/R, Excel)
